# Redline

Paste a link, keep the audio, listen like a podcast — in a black, white and red telemetry style,
with a turntable player you seek by dragging around its ring of ticks.

Installable web app (iPhone / Android). Downloads happen on the phone with a-Shell + yt-dlp through
a Shortcut, then come in with Import. Audio, marks and notes live on the device.

`public/` is the app (no build step). `node serve.mjs` serves it locally.
